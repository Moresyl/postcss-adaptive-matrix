# Code of conduct

**English** · [简体中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

We are committed to a friendly, harassment-free environment for everyone taking part, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Use welcoming, inclusive and professional language;
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences;
- Accept constructive feedback, and keep the good of the community in view;
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and work to repair the effects.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language, unwanted attention, or harassment;
- Personal attacks, insults, demeaning remarks, or inflammatory language;
- Publishing someone's private information without permission;
- Sustained disruption of discussions, code review, or collaboration on the project;
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject issues, comments, commits and other contributions that breach this code, and may restrict participation temporarily or permanently. To raise a security or conduct concern privately, contact the maintainers through the repository's Security page.
